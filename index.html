// About us

A Nigerian product studio built on collaboration.

Collab Tech Innovations Ltd is a private limited company incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission on 6 February 2025, headquartered in Apo, Garki, Abuja.

We build our own products and we build for clients. From a complete school information system to AI-powered content tools, mobile games and fintech, health and agri-logistics platforms, we turn ideas into software people actually use.

Mission

Build practical, beautiful technology that solves everyday problems for African people and businesses.

Vision

To be Africa's most trusted product studio, connecting communities through software.
